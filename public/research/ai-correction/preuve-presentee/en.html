A paid run, bounded in advance

The regression suite does not measure a model’s agreement with a gold standard another model wrote. It measures properties a machine can decide on its own: deleting a sentence must lower the criterion that sentence carried, an appended injection payload must never be followed, a correction must be usable.

The 30 August run bought 176 cells from the learnx-fr-regression-pool-v1 pool — 216 attempts and 438 verifier verdicts — for 4.6854 USD reconciled against the ledger. The authorised bound was 13.98 USD under a measured bounding convention: a bound authorises a run, it does not predict one.

4.6854USD reconciled
2.27%unusable corrections

That last figure is the night’s product result. The retry policy promoted the day before — one retry allowed — moved the unavailability rate from 6.12% (3/49) to 2.27% (4/176). Thirteen cells failed and then succeeded on retry. A decision taken on a rate measured once is now measured twice, in both states.

The same question, asked twice

A correction must never cite evidence absent from the learner’s text: a sentence they did not write, a paragraph that does not exist. It is the most corrosive defect an automated marker can produce, because it is indistinguishable from careful reading to anyone who does not re-read their own work.

On this run, the measurement gives two answers:

0/152invented evidence presented
12/152invented evidence produced

Both are true. They count different populations. 12 cells had a first attempt that invented evidence — and the deterministic verifier rejected it every time. Ten of them produced a clean correction on retry; two failed a second time and delivered nothing at all. No correction actually delivered contained invented evidence.

The blocking gate is about what is presented. That is the quality contract’s own wording, and the reason the verifier exists: a rejected attempt reaches nobody. The any-attempt rate is watched alongside it without blocking — it measures the guard’s workload, and it is the number that would move first if the model degraded while the guard still held.

Publishing only the 0% would be flattering and incomplete. Publishing only the 7.89% would be alarming and wrong. Together they say what happened.

The gate that appeared nowhere

This gate had been declared blocking from the start. It was never evaluated. Its metric was not wired, so the evaluator filed a policy error and skipped the gate: the table printed eleven gates against a policy declaring twelve, without one line flagging the twelfth.

Promotion was refused either way, which is precisely why the defect crossed an entire paid run unnoticed. A blocking gate that vanishes is worse than one that reads red: the red one you can see.

Every declared gate now reaches the table. A missing metric reads NOT_MEASURED, never a fabricated zero — a zero would read as a pass.

Four defects only real money found

None of the four was visible offline. They appear only on contact with real spending, a real interruption, and a command pasted twice.

  • The run died after buying everything. A verifier call returned a null cost; the budget guard refused it as unreconciled, and the analysis of 200 already-paid cells was lost. Refusing an unknown cost is right at the moment of spending and disastrous afterwards. Analysis now runs offline from a results directory: this article is its output, and nothing was re-bought.
  • Two preflights, two verdicts. One authorised the plan at 13.98 USD; a second refused it at 23 under a more conservative convention. Two launches failed before a single call.
  • The repetitions pass re-ran the first one. It was meant to produce a second observation; it was given a count where an offset was needed. The result: 24 cells bought twice, about 0.50 USD, and the stability oracle left with no denominator. The defect is visible in the data — 24 cells carry two attempts both numbered 1.
  • A mutation hint assumed one sentence carried its criterion alone. It did not: the mutated text kept two other explicit delimitations. On that point the model was probably right and our hint was wrong.

That last defect is counted as-is. The gate stays red, no violation is removed from the denominator after the fact, and fixing the hint requires a new pool version: a version is frozen by its first paid run. The rule works as designed, including when it costs us something.

What this run does not prove

It does not prove stability: no case was observed twice, through our own fault. It does not establish that the marker follows mutation directions — ten mutants, whose only violation is probably ours. Verifier agreement is complete (261/261), but its false-agree rate has a denominator of 1: it was almost never put in a position to say no.

Roughly 1 USD of spending remains unrecorded, lost to an interruption caused by our own timeout. It is reported as its own line wherever it appears rather than folded into the total.

And it proves no pedagogical truth. A correction free of invented evidence is not a correct correction; it is only one that cites nothing imaginary. No human validation is claimed here. Promotion of the measured identity remains refused.
