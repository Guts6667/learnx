Gate 4: when a failure reveals an oracle limitation.

The gate stopped exactly as planned. Yet the observed disagreement does not show an obvious model error: it reveals a missing category in our own evidence vocabulary.

A small, bounded, non-replayable gate

The learnx-writing-fr-sonnet-5-evidence-assist-v3@1.0.0 identity allowed at most four calls to Sonnet 5 through the Anthropic route, with no retry or fallback and a USD 0.251136 total ceiling. Stop-on-first-failure was preregistered. Mutation, injection, panel and holdout remained closed.

The positive case reached 9/9 relations. The negative case reached 7/9. Cost was fully reconciled, with no retry or fallback. The procedural verdict is NO_GO_SEMANTIC_DISAGREEMENT / CAMPAIGN_CLOSED / NO_REPLAY.

The model may not have been wrong

The negative response explicitly said “without choosing” and “I make no recommendation”. The pseudo-oracle expected NOT_DEMONSTRATED, allowing only abstention or omission. Sonnet linked those passages to EVIDENCE_AGAINST_ELEMENT.

That relation is semantically plausible: the response does not merely fail to demonstrate the element, it explicitly states the opposite. The stop remains valid because the mapping was frozen, but the gap exposes an incomplete ontology rather than an obvious pedagogical error.

A useful experiment can fail while showing that the measuring instrument needs improvement.

What we cannot claim

  • The injection case was never sent: no injection resistance is proven for this identity.
  • The 10×2 panel and holdout were not opened.
  • Total cost is known, but not the input, cache, reasoning and output breakdown.
  • Two calls cannot assess Sonnet 5’s general quality.

Next evidence

Before another call, LearnX must distinguish SUPPORTED, EXPLICITLY_REFUTED, NOT_DEMONSTRATED and AMBIGUOUS. Minimal pairs must test silence, abstention, explicit negation, internal contradiction and positive evidence. Any change will create a new identity and a new article; this one will remain unchanged.

Current state

0 pipeline promoted, 0 public correction contract, 0 progression effect. The gate is closed and will not be rewritten.
