Researching evidence instead of producing a verdict.

Gemini and Sonnet 5 showed that semantic understanding is not enough when the model must also copy exact quotations and respect a technical budget. LearnX therefore took control of passages.

Gemini: correct meaning, rejected quotation

Gemini passed a three-case gate: 27 elements covered, with compliant negative and injection cases. The 10×2 panel then produced 10 valid workflows out of 20 planned, covering five cases twice. The 11th call proposed a non-exact quotation; LearnX rejected it and the campaign stopped. Reconciled cost: USD 0.04345875.

This was not a pedagogical verdict on Gemini. It showed that asking the model to recover exact text and offsets remained a source of invalidity.

Sonnet 5: good screening, insufficient request profiles

Screening passed 3/3 cases, 27/27 statuses and 17 exact quotations. In the panel, ten workflows were valid; the 11th call consumed 2,500 reasoning tokens without visible output. A new identity capped at 1,024 reasoning tokens still exceeded that limit by 58 tokens on its first call. Both campaigns stopped without claiming Sonnet had poor pedagogical quality.

The change: passages created by LearnX

LearnX now segments the response before network dispatch. Each passage receives an opaque identifier, offsets and a hash. The model no longer copies a quotation or proposes a level: it only links an authorised passage to a rubric element with a “for”, “against” or “abstain” relation.

The output remains a candidate. LearnX validates identifier, ownership, safety, schema and coherence before any use.

What the model can no longer do

  • produce a score, final level or pass/fail;
  • change progression;
  • freely write published feedback;
  • quote a passage that was not pre-segmented;
  • trigger a retry or unreconciled cost.

What still had to be proven

This architecture reduced the error surface, but had not yet passed the four-case gate. The researcher role was therefore neither promoted nor activated. The next article documents this new identity’s first semantic stop.

Correct reading

Transport compatibility and pedagogical quality are separate axes. A successful smoke is not a selection.
