Composite pipeline

Why two models do not manufacture truth.

Having one model verify another broadens risk coverage, but the second model can introduce its own severity.

15 August 2026Conditional simulationMistral + Sonnet

Result

The simulation yields 64 non-uncertain results out of 72 and 8 uncertain states. It avoids false favourable outcomes, but retains three false negatives and turns disagreements into abstentions.

Simulated pipeline coverage

Publishable results64
Uncertain8

Simulation over 72 historical corpus cells; not a production result.

Trade-off

Average simulated cost is below Sonnet alone because verification is conditional. However, the P90 workflow reserve can be higher and pedagogical signal worsened.

DecisionMistral + Sonnet composite v1 remains a pedagogical NO-GO. No vote, average or third model should manufacture truth.

What remains useful

Disagreement carries information: it signals a case where LearnX should abstain. But models should no longer be the authority assigning final levels.
