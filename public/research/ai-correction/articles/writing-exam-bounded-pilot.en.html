Final exam & product decision

A scientific NO-GO followed by a bounded product pilot.

The benchmark verdict is not rewritten. Product separately accepts a limited, monitored version with no effect on learning progression.

24 August 2026Writing · fr-FRSonnet 4.6 · Anthropic

Why a new Writing exam

The four-family campaign mixed writing, reflection, practice and project. Its review confirmed an eliminatory Practice defect and contestable golds. LearnX therefore preregistered a Writing-only exam with two independent autonomous authoring processes, agreement measured before selection, a fresh corpus and rules frozen before any call.

Technically clean execution

The sealed exam contains 24 cases repeated three times. All 72 primary calls and six targeted second passes returned valid structures. No retry, transport incident, unknown cost, fabricated evidence or followed injection was observed.

The pedagogical gates still failed

  • criterion agreement: 80.19%, below the 85% minimum;
  • 7 false passes, above the allowed maximum of 0;
  • 1 two-level ordinal gap, above the allowed maximum of 0.

The false passes cluster on three repetitions of an ambiguous explanation, three repetitions of an erroneous action plan and one repetition of an ambiguous action plan. A guard triggered by the model’s own score cannot always detect its own leniency.

Final scientific verdictNO-GO. The corpus is consumed, thresholds were not changed after results and humanReviewApproved remains false.

The product decision is separate

The owner authorises a bounded V4 despite the NO-GO, to learn from real use without claiming universal accuracy. This is not a scientific promotion of the model.

  • writing/fr-FR only, text and low risk;
  • strictly formative, criterion-by-criterion feedback;
  • indicative score only, never used to validate mastery;
  • an unreliable criterion returns “revise this part”, with no exact global score;
  • Sonnet 4.6 and the Anthropic route are pinned; the same model performs the targeted second pass;
  • complimentary credits only during the pilot; no public sale of AI correction;
  • no AI result writes to learning progression.

What we will monitor

Two known defects become explicit indicators: a hard constraint can be mentioned without affecting the level, and the score guard can miss overrating. A fresh exam is required before expanding scope or opening sales.

Limited promiseAutonomous, criteria-based formative feedback grounded in the learner’s response — never certified correction or validation by AI.
