01 · Current decision

Stop asking a model to decide the grade.

The campaigns showed that useful models can remain unstable at boundaries and propagate one weakness across several criteria. LearnX now formalises the rubric: models locate or challenge evidence, then versioned rules determine the levels.

AI finds the evidence. LearnX executes the rubric and abstains when the result cannot be determined honestly.

Implemented offline. The first French WRITING archetype contains three criteria and nine atomic elements. Its compiler, mechanical oracle and Gemini evidence-research campaign are ready, but no real call is authorised.

02 · Understanding the method

Smoke, mini-panel, full and 24×3: what do they mean?

Each stage answers a different question. Passing an initial test only proves that the model can communicate with the system; it is never enough to select it.

Definitions of benchmark stages
TermPlain-language definitionWhat it demonstrates
Smoke testA very short test using one representative case. It checks that the provider route, model and response format work together.The model may proceed to the next test. Its pedagogical quality is not yet demonstrated.
Smoke sequenceThree sequential cases: a clearly successful answer, an ambiguous answer and a prompt-injection attempt. Testing stops at the first material failure.The model appears technically compatible and safe enough for a small panel.
Mini-panelA small predefined set, usually six varied answers, subjected to a blind pedagogical review separate from execution.It quickly exposes grading, tone or rubric errors before an expensive campaign.
Full panelThe complete campaign across the development corpus. Here, it covers all 24 calibrated answers.It measures all planned task families, but not yet generalisation to unseen cases.
24×3The same 24 answers are assessed three times, producing 72 logical runs. Repetition does not inflate the sample; it measures stability.It reveals both average errors and changes in judgement on an identical answer.
Sealed holdoutA separate corpus never used to tune the prompt or select thresholds. It remains closed until the configuration is frozen.It tests whether the solution generalises without having learned the development answers.

Example. Two successful smokes do not equal a full panel. And 24×3 still represents only 24 semantic situations: repetitions measure stability. The new method separates a mechanical oracle, a synthetic pseudo-oracle and unlabelled real-world shadow data.

05 · Selected architecture

Turn the rubric into an executable program.

Assessment is no longer a vote between models. A researcher proposes evidence, an optional independent falsifier challenges it without seeing a grade, then LearnX applies the atomic rules and issues a reconstructible certificate.

Step 1

Rubric

Elements, owners, rules and remediation are compiled.

Step 2

Research

Gemini proposes statuses and exact spans, without a level or score.

Step 3

Falsification

A separate role looks for contradiction, omission or ambiguity.

Step 4

Calculation

LearnX verifies evidence and executes the level rules.

Step 5

Certificate

Authored feedback, revision or clarification; no progression effect.

Every authorised resolution of an ambiguity is evaluated. If the level remains unchanged, feedback may be delivered. If it changes, LearnX withholds the exact score and asks one targeted clarification.

06 · Success criteria

Block structural defects before measuring a model.

The compiler must first prove that evidence cannot be counted twice, every level is reachable and adding valid evidence cannot lower a result.

Hard gates

  • No span outside the learner answer or canary leak.
  • No unknown element or wrong owner.
  • No double penalty or non-monotonic rule.
  • No exact score under material ambiguity.
  • Levels and feedback computed by LearnX.
  • No effect on progression.

Monitored indicators

  • False SUPPORTED and false NOT_DEMONSTRATED.
  • Coverage and abstention on clear cases.
  • Locality and monotonicity of minimal pairs.
  • Variability by element family.
  • Recovered incidents and unavailable results.
  • P50/P90 cost per publishable correction.
07 · Evidence still required

What remains to be demonstrated before beta.

The offline engine exists. We now need to show that Gemini finds reliable elements, then measure whether a falsifier genuinely improves the certificate.

1. Rehearse P0 on a disposable Neon branch

Validate CALL_INTENT, idempotent recovery and missing-cost reconciliation without touching production.

2. Author the semantic WRITING corpus

Keep the executable mechanical oracle separate from the synthetic pseudo-oracle used to measure extraction.

3. Run Gemini researcher 10×2

Pin route, profile and budget; require 20/20 workflows and 100% span and safety checks, with no model-produced level or score.

4. Measure the benefit of a falsifier

Compare on the same outputs, without voting or exposing a grade to the second model. Keep it only if it reduces false statuses.

5. Keep the holdout sealed

The holdout remains one-shot and opens only after engine, researcher and expanded-development gates pass.
