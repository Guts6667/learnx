First benchmark: a good average is not enough.

Our first campaign compared models acting as judges. It mostly showed that a strong aggregate score can hide exactly the failures LearnX does not want to publish.

Summary

The corpus contained 24 synthetic French responses: four activity families, six response profiles and three repetitions per candidate. No model was promoted. Gemini scored well overall but failed the injection-safety, output-validity and second-pass gates. Terra could not execute the tested structured contract. Sonnet measurements had to be repeated after a persistence failure.

The average describes a campaign. It does not protect a learner from a rare but serious failure.

What we measured

Each response was evaluated against authored criteria. The benchmark separated criterion agreement, fabricated quotations, invalid output, injection, calibration, latency, variability and costs. Repetitions measured stability; they did not create 72 independent pedagogical situations.

Gemini’s misleading signal

Gemini 3.6 Flash reached 90.40% criterion agreement, 0% fabricated quotations, 13.89% calibration error and 4.55% variability. Yet injection safety was only 50%, invalid output reached 8.33%, and second-pass agreement was 81.82%. Those three gates were enough to stop promotion.

This changed how we read benchmarks: average quality can never compensate for a blocking safety, evidence or format failure.

What this campaign does not prove

  • The corpus is synthetic and French.
  • Results depend on the pinned prompt, schema, route and model.
  • A technical failure does not prove poor intrinsic pedagogical quality.
  • Three repetitions measure stability, not real-world diversity.

Decision

We selected no judge. The next step was to audit the gold labels, criteria and second-pass decisions before spending more. This leads to the second article: blind review of the protocol itself.

Research status

Retrospective article based on preserved artefacts. No pipeline promoted and no effect on progression.
