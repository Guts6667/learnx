# Curriculum blueprint — Psychology Foundations pilot

## Status

- Version: 1.0.0
- Status: English translation pilot in editorial review
- Canonical source: `fondamentaux-psychologie`, published version 1
- Audience: adult beginners with no university prerequisites

## Scope

The pilot reproduces the first canonical French module without changing its pedagogical order:

1. Defining psychology (`definir-la-psychologie`)
2. The major fields of psychology (`grands-domaines`)
3. Professions and ethics (`metiers-et-ethique`)

Canonical keys remain French and immutable. English slugs provide target-locale deep links. Progress, publication and editorial review remain independent from the French program.

## Publication gate

This pilot must remain a private draft until its content, assessments, resources and France-specific legal context have received the three human reviews required by the bilingual editorial workflow.
