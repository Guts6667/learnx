# Curriculum blueprint — Psychology Foundations pilot

## Status

- Version: 1.1.0
- Status: progressive English translation in editorial review
- Canonical source: `fondamentaux-psychologie`, published version 1
- Audience: adult beginners with no university prerequisites

## Scope

The current translation reproduces the first two canonical French stages
without changing their pedagogical order.

Stage 1 — Discovering the discipline:

1. Defining psychology (`definir-la-psychologie`)
2. The major fields of psychology (`grands-domaines`)
3. Professions and ethics (`metiers-et-ethique`)

Stage 2 — Understanding the major schools:

1. The birth of experimental psychology (`naissance-psychologie-experimentale`)
2. Behaviorism and learning (`behaviorisme-apprentissage`)
3. Cognitivism, humanism and psychoanalysis (`courants-modernes`)

Canonical keys remain French and immutable. English slugs provide target-locale deep links. Progress, publication and editorial review remain independent from the French program.

## Publication gate

Each translated lot must remain a private draft until its content, assessments,
resources and cultural or legal context have received the human reviews required
by the bilingual editorial workflow.
